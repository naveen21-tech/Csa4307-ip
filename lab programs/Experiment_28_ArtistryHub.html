About the artist

A life spent chasing light

Emily Harris (b. 1978) is a contemporary painter whose luminous, layered canvases explore memory, landscape and the passage of time. Raised on the coast and trained in fine art, she blends traditional oil technique with digital experimentation. Her influences range from the Impressionists to modern colour-field painters.

Her work has been exhibited in more than 30 solo and group shows and is held in private and public collections. (Demo content for the lab – Emily Harris is a fictional artist.)
